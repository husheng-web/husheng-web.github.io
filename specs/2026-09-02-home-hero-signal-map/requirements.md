# Home hero signal map — requirements

## Goal

Use the `frontend-design` skill to give the bilingual portfolio home a more distinctive, subject-grounded opening: recruiters should see the owner's product-building method as a concise system before they scroll to the detailed process section.

## Observable behavior

- The home hero includes a six-stage, read-only system map derived solely from the existing localized `buildSteps` content.
- It appears as a compact process signal on wide screens and remains readable, ordered, and non-overflowing on small screens.
- It does not introduce personal claims, project facts, or decorative data.
- It respects reduced-motion preferences and preserves the current keyboard/navigation behavior.

## Constraints

- Preserve the rational editorial, restrained-futurism direction and the existing token system.
- Orange remains a small signal only; no new large accent surface or gradient is introduced.
- The process map supplements rather than replaces the detailed build-process section.

## Out of scope

- New routes, media, analytics, contact information, or content-model changes.
