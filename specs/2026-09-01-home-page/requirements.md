# Home page requirements

## Goal

Implement the bilingual portfolio home as an evidence-first narrative: identity, selected work, method, capability structure, truthful proof placeholder, About entry point, and route-based finale.

## Constraints

- Use the frozen design and motion systems.
- Keep project facts, translations, media, contact data, and experience content pending unless already supplied.
- Do not implement Phase 4 or other page bodies.

## Acceptance

- `/zh` and `/en` render all seven home sections.
- Featured work comes only from `featuredProjects`.
- Pending projects and personal information remain visibly pending.
- The page supports keyboard, reduced motion, and the target responsive widths.
